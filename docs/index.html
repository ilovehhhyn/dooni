01 Your to-think list.

Every chat has a thoughts tab for ideas, questions, and prompts you’re not ready to ask. Jot them down, click to copy, and check them off. Save now. Ask later.

02 Catch a thought.

Highlight something worth keeping. A shortcut saves it straight to that chat’s to-think list.

03 Your chats, together.

Prompts from Codex and Claude surface automatically. Keep what you’ve asked beside what you want to think about next.
